# Acknowledgements

PaperAudit was developed as an independent academic-manuscript auditing framework.

Its attention to syntactic clarity, vague agents, metaphorical verbs, unnecessary rhetorical framing, and information density was partly inspired by the open-source **yomiyasu** project by nanaism:

https://github.com/nanaism/yomiyasu

`yomiyasu` is distributed under the MIT License.

PaperAudit differs in purpose and design. It focuses on academic manuscripts and separates:

- research specificity;
- evidentiary grounding;
- AI-style pattern risk;
- readability and precision;
- academic-integrity constraints.

PaperAudit does not attempt to infer AI authorship or optimize writing against AI-detection systems.
